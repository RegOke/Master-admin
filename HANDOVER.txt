PRODUCT CONTEXT
Advoc8 is a stakeholder/public-affairs product. Users work with people and
organisations, contact details, political information, staff, activities, feeds,
agenda items, notes and custom fields. Reg is the first design hire, building a
shared Figma design system and prototypes for developer handoff.

There are two sources of contact information:
1. Advoc8-managed master details, maintained centrally by Advoc8 staff through
   admin screens and shown on stakeholder profiles.
2. Customer-added custom details, maintained by the customer's team and visible
   within that team. They supplement master details and should not silently
   overwrite or become part of the central master record.

Important terminology: use “Custom details” in the customer-facing prototype.
Earlier iterations used “Private details”; Reg explicitly changed this.
“Only visible to team” is the visibility message, not “Only visible to you”.

WHY ADMIN IS BEING EXPLORED
Current master admin uses fixed email slots (Email, Email 2, Email 3, Email 4)
and fixed work/mobile phone fields. Addresses are separate cards on the right.
The customer custom-details feature supports individual entries, labels and
addresses. Will proposed making master contact details work in the same way,
so managed and customer-added details can be displayed together consistently.

An MVP can leave master admin as it is and ship customer custom details first.
Admin parity is a separate scope decision, not a decision already agreed here.
Harry raised exports as a consideration if labels are unrestricted. Email labels
being optional was discussed, but was not conclusively decided in the supplied
conversation. Phone types already include work/mobile. Addresses currently have
both a name and a type; preserve that distinction until a new design changes it.

Possible future admin work: replace fixed contact slots with repeatable email
and phone entries, supporting add/edit/remove and optional descriptive labels;
reuse address editing patterns. Keep user-specific custom details separate from
centrally managed contacts. Confirm label/export decisions with the team rather
than inventing rules in a prototype.

DESIGN SYSTEM AND INTERACTION PREFERENCES
Use existing Advoc8 UI patterns. Reg strongly prefers few steps, compact default
states, minimal copy and no duplicate buttons, repeated notices or extra panels.
Most customers will not add custom contacts, so the empty state should be compact.
Do not add unsolicited alternative versions or recommendations to the prototype.
Include a close icon in modal headers. Align card actions consistently to the
right. Tooltips appear on hover/focus; informative icons keep the normal cursor.
Tooltip arrows point towards their trigger. Preserve the rest of each page.

Typography: Cerebri Sans, available in Figma via uploaded fonts. Body Default and
Small have Regular, SemiBold and Bold styles. Button Small/Default/Large now use
Cerebri Sans Medium across Primary/Secondary/Danger; control/input styles remain
separate. For exact HTML reproduction use the captured product CSS, not guessed
Figma-to-CSS measurements.

Figma components use auto layout, shared variables/styles, boolean visibility
properties and instance swaps. Avoid redundant size/style variants. Icons use
padded 20px masters with sizing centralised in Icon slot; sizes include 12/16/20/24.
There are independent swappable trailing icons for form inputs (up to two).
The small triangle is searchable as triangle/caret/dropdown; small x is searchable
as clear/small x/cross/xmark/remove. Both scale and remain centred.
